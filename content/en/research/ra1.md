## Models that understand the mechanisms

Process-based models separate the drivers – climate, land use, pollution, harvesting and natural succession – and how they interact. That allows them to say something about conditions we have not seen before.

The drivers act together, not one by one. That is why we deliver forecasts with uncertainty, not single answers.

## What happens to nature if we choose differently?

The forecasts are linked to SNOW, the socio-economic model of Statistics Norway, which covers more than 40 industries, and broken down to municipal level. This lets us compare scenarios for energy, food and raw materials against nature.

## Tested against reality

Forecasts are compared with the observed state of ecosystems, and the models are adjusted. Model, monitoring, comparison and adjustment run in a loop.
