## Citizen science where it matters most

Volunteer observations are directed to where the models need data most. Species observations are the backbone. We develop training and guidance for municipalities and organisations that want to use citizen science in planning and monitoring.

## Education, dialogue and meeting places

- Master's and PhD courses and continuing education
- [The Gjærevoll Conference](/en/news/gjaerevoll-conference-2027/)
- An open digital platform with maps and scenarios
- An exhibition at the NTNU University Museum
- A pilot in Central Norway followed through the 2027–2031 electoral term
