## A common yardstick for nature

We will build indicators that link functionally from genes to species, to communities and ecosystems, anchored in **Essential Biodiversity Variables (EBVs)**.

## Measures that fit reporting

The same measures should be usable in natural capital accounting, in the Red List and in the reporting requirements businesses face. Today they diverge.

| Framework | What it is |
|---|---|
| Ecosystem accounting (SEEA-EA) | UN standard for ecosystem accounts |
| Red List (IUCN) | Assessment of species' extinction risk |
| TNFD and SBTN | Frameworks for nature risk and nature targets in business |
| CSRD | EU sustainability reporting requirements |
| Genetic indicators | Measures of genetic diversity under the Global Biodiversity Framework |

## What do we compare with?

Not "untouched nature", but the expected trend without local impacts. The difference between the expected and the actual trend is the effect of what we can actually change.
