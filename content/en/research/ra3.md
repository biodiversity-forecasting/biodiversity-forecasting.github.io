## The translation zone

Between research and decisions lies a translation zone: shared maps and dashboards that different actors read in their own way, researchers who speak the language of planners and businesses, and users who shape the next version.

Forecasts, targets and data from RA1, RA2 and RA4 are co-created into practical tools for **plans, budgets, reports and investments**.

## NaturA 2050: what happens if we choose A or B?

Dashboards and foresight tools that combine forecasts, targets and data. Nature, energy, housing, transport and outdoor recreation in the same trade-off.

## Policy instruments and nature risk

**For policy and management:** Forecasts linked to nature taxes, no net land take and nature budgets. Statistics Norway's SNOW model calculates what the instruments cost and deliver.

**For business and finance:** Nature risk and double materiality – how a company affects nature, and how nature loss affects the company. In collaboration with DNV.
