Research at CBF is organised in five research areas (work packages). They form a chain: the **information arena** gathers and models data, the **forecasts** show how nature develops under different choices, the **indicators** make the results comparable, and **from forecast to action** turns them into tools. The **knowledge arena** runs across all of them and makes sure users help shape the next version.

Forecasts are tested against reality in a loop: model, monitoring, comparison and adjustment.
