The centre's tools must work in practice, not only in models. That is why they are developed and tested in two regions with long data time series and strong pressure on nature.

## Central Norway

The Trondheimsfjord area and the Trøndelag coast have long observation series and strong pressure on land and sea. User partners such as Høylandet and Frøya municipalities, Trøndelag County Authority and businesses help shape the tools.

A pilot in Central Norway will be followed throughout the 2027–2031 electoral term.

## Finnmark

Finnmark also has long time series and strong pressure on land. The Climate-ecological Observatory for Arctic Tundra (COAT) and the Finnmark Estate (FeFo) are key partners.

## Who is involved

Municipalities, aquaculture, forestry, agriculture, finance, energy and NGOs. See the full list under [People and partners](/en/people/#partnarar).
