Under the title "Forecasts for nature – a basis for better decisions", we presented how the centre will give nature what the climate field already has: forecasts for different choices, common accounting and indicators across sectors.

The Norwegian Environment Agency is part of the centre's reference group, together with NVE and the Norwegian Agriculture Agency.
